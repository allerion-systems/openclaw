Allerion

Your personal AI assistant — gateway, channels, plugins, and control panel. Install it on your own machine, or use the hosted instance.

macOS / Linux / WSL

Requires Node.js 22.19+ and git.

curl -fsSL --proto '=https' --tlsv1.2 https://allerion.io/install.sh | bash

Windows (PowerShell)

Requires Node.js 22.19+ and git.

iwr -useb https://allerion.io/install.ps1 | iex

Hosted

Prefer not to install? Use the hosted control panel at jolly.allerion.io, or talk to your assistant in Allerion Chat.

After installing

openclaw onboard

Walks you through connecting your model provider and channels.
